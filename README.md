# **1. The Notebook (2004)**

*A timeless love story about two people from different backgrounds who fight to stay together despite life's challenges.*

**Genre:** Romance, Drama

**Rating:**  ★★★★★★★★☆☆ (8/10)

**Running Time:** 2h 3m

**Why I Like It:** I like this movie because it tells a powerful and emotional love story. The strong connection between the main characters and their determination to stay together despite challenges make the film inspiring and memorable.

# **2. Me Before You (2016)**

*A touching romance between a cheerful young woman and a man whose life changes after an accident. Emotional and inspiring.*

**Genre:** Romance, Drama

**Rating:** ★★★★★★★★☆☆ (7/10)

# **3. To All the Boys I've Loved Before (2018)**

*A fun and sweet romantic comedy about a high school girl whose secret love letters are unexpectedly sent out. Lighthearted and heartwarming.*

**Genre:** Romance, Comedy

**Rating:** ★★★★★★★★★☆ (9/10)

**Running Time:** 1h 39m
